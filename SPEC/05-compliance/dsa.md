# Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065)

Assessed in dossier FSTP-DOS-DA-DSA v1.0.0 (10 October 2026). Gap IDs G-12 to G-22 refer to that dossier.

## Position

- The SaaS is a hosting service. CrabNebula Ltd is its provider. Self-hosted cells make the tenant, not CrabNebula, the potential provider.
- Online platform status depends on the cross-tenant directory of open calls (P-76), the public MCP tools and the template library. Public subgrant pages are a minor and purely ancillary feature (G-17).
- Whether CrabNebula Ltd is a micro or small enterprise decides most online platform duties and the transparency reports (L-16).

## Duties to add to the specification

- Points of contact for authorities and recipients, protected from white-labelling (G-14).
- Terms and conditions covering restrictions, content moderation policy and automated tools (G-15).
- A public notice and action mechanism (G-12) and statements of reasons (G-13).
- A procedure for notifying life or safety threatening offences (G-16).
- Tenant tooling for tenants that are providers themselves (G-21).
