# AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)

## Position (O-02, accepted)

Subgrant evaluation as designed is not an Annex III use case. Findings are advisory, and a named human records every decision. If someone argues otherwise, the Article 6(3) reasoning for preparatory or narrow procedural tasks is the fallback.

## Design outcomes

- Findings are advisory and every decision has a named human (I-10, R-LA-03).
- Applicants are told AI assessment is used, and the pre-check discloses AI interaction (R-LA-08). See Article 50.
- AI literacy measures for reviewers using findings (Article 4).
- Traces retained to support contestation (R-LA-07).

## Reassessment triggers

Repeat the classification if any of these change:

- findings or bands feed a score, threshold or ranking without human entry,
- a tenant uses the platform for a purpose listed in Annex III, for example allocating public assistance benefits on behalf of a public authority,
- P-28 indicative bands are switched on and reviewers can no longer show independent scoring.
- a programme allows agents to record scores (P-77) and reviewers delegate scoring to an AI system rather than deciding themselves.
