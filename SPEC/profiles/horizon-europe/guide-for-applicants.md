## 5. How to apply

1. Create an account on {{platform.url}}.
2. Draft your proposal in the online form. Only the information needed for evaluation is requested at this stage.
3. Optionally run the pre-submission check. An AI system reviews your draft for completeness, quality and accuracy against the call rubric and returns findings, never scores. You can run it {{P-23.per_day}} per day and {{P-23.per_call}} per call.
4. Submit before the deadline. You can withdraw at any time before signing a contract.

Your drafts and pre-check results are visible only to your team.

## 8. Support programme

Alongside your funding, the consortium offers:

- **Mentoring** by a consortium expert, if you want it.
- **A chance to present your results** to the consortium.
- **Promotion** of your project through consortium channels, within the publication choice you make.
- **Guidance on the Cyber Resilience Act** as it applies to your software.

This support is free. It is not part of your grant amount and never affects payments or later evaluations. Your mentor will not review your future proposals or approve your payment requests. You can end mentoring at any time.

No consortium partner will make you commercial offers through this programme.

## 14. Complaints

You can contest how a decision was reached, for example a missed step, a conflict of interest or a deviation from this Guide. Disagreement with reviewers' expert scores is not grounds for a complaint.

1. File your complaint in the platform within {{P-61}} of the decision notice.
2. A member of the coordinator's team who took no part in the decision replies within {{P-62}}.
3. If you are not satisfied, you can escalate within {{P-63}}. At least two members of the independent review committee review your case and reply within {{P-64}}.

Filing a complaint never affects your evaluation or payments. If your complaint is upheld, your proposal is re-evaluated by different reviewers, or re-enters the next call at the stage where the error occurred.
