# Guide for Applicants

Every open call publishes a Guide for Applicants. The platform generates it from the frozen call policy and the funder profile, and publishes it with the call on the public page, as accessible HTML and as a PDF.

## Required sections

| Section | Content | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| About the programme | Programme objectives and scope | Programme narrative |
| Call at a glance | Call identifier, opening date, deadline with time zone, call budget, grant range, maximum per recipient, language | P-04, P-05, P-25, call record |
| What we fund | Closed list of eligible activity types, expected results, open licence and open access requirements | P-55, P-26 |
| Who can apply | Persons and categories supported, tie-break priority, minors and guardian consent, exclusions, repeat applicants | P-13, P-17, I-15, R-SB-02 |
| How to apply | Account creation, proposal form, pre-submission check and its limits, AI disclosure, submission, withdrawal | P-23, R-LA-08, R-SB-03 |
| How proposals are evaluated | Knock-out criteria, criteria and weights, pass threshold, stage 2, ranking and cut-off, independent review, reviewer anonymity, conflict of interest rules, notification deadlines | P-01 to P-03, P-07 to P-11, P-15, P-16 |
| How the amount is set | Criteria for calculating the exact amount, budget changes in stage 2, ineligible costs | Annexe section 6, P-04, P-05 |
| Support programme | What grantees receive beyond funding | P-60 |
| Financial support to third parties | What FSTP means, that recipients are not parties to the grant agreement, obligations passed on to recipients, the action period limit, data shared with the granting authority, the granting authority's use of materials | `05-compliance/horizon-europe-mga.md` |
| Contract and payments | Contract instrument, signature cascade, milestones, payment requests and evidence, payment on delivery, payment timing, bank detail changes, hardship contributions | P-29, P-30, P-34, P-36, P-59, P-27 |
| Visibility and publication | EU funding acknowledgement duties, public listing opt-in | P-50, P-51, L-08 |
| Data protection and confidentiality | Data collected at each stage, retention, rights, Portal Privacy Statement, confidentiality of proposals | `05-compliance/gdpr.md`, P-41 |
| Timeline | Every date and window in one table | Timeline engine |
| Complaints | How to contest a procedural decision, levels, deadlines and remedies | `02-domain/complaints.md`, P-61 to P-64 |
| Contact and FAQ | Contact channel, frequently asked questions | Programme narrative |

## Rules

- **I-51** Every parameter value in the Guide equals the frozen call policy. Parameter-bound fields cannot be edited by hand.
- **I-52** The Guide is generated only when every required section is present, including the five MGA 6.2.D.1 elements (I-48) and the flow-down obligations (I-49).
- **I-53** A published Guide version is immutable. A correction is a numbered corrigendum, published with the call and notified to every account that started a proposal in that call.
- Narrative sections (programme, support programme, FAQ) are editable before publication and follow I-53 afterwards.
- A corrigendum can never change a frozen parameter (I-12).
- Statements about grant agreement obligations use wording approved in legal review. The template marks each one with `TODO(legal)` until then.

The Horizon Europe template is `profiles/horizon-europe/guide-for-applicants.md`.
