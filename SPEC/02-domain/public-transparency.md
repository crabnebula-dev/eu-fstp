# Public transparency

Each programme has a public section showing open calls, funded subgrants and finished subgrants.

## What is shown

| Section | Visibility | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open calls | Always public | Call text, deadlines, criteria and weights, eligibility, budget, applicant guide, how to apply |
| Funded subgrants | Opt-in by the grantee, up to P-50 | Fields per the chosen level, from GRANTED onwards |
| Finished subgrants | Opt-in by the grantee, up to P-51 | Fields per the chosen level, plus accepted deliverables where the grantee includes them |

## Visibility levels

| Level | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Hidden | Nothing about this grant. Default for every grant |
| Project | Project title, public summary, call, status |
| Project and grantee | Adds the grantee name |
| Full | Adds the granted amount and milestone status |

Finished subgrants can also show accepted deliverables with their licences and evidence snapshots, if the grantee includes them.

## Opt-in model

- The programme sets a maximum level for funded (P-50) and finished (P-51) subgrants.
- Each grantee chooses a level up to that maximum, separately for the funded and finished phases. The default is Hidden.
- The grantee can change the choice at any time. Narrowing or withdrawing applies immediately to pages, the feed and caches.
- Aggregate figures (number of grants, total amount per call) are shown only when they cover at least P-54 grants, so no single grant can be inferred.

## Rules

- **I-41** No public page shows anything about a proposal that never reached GRANTED.
- **I-42** No public page names a minor, at any visibility level.
- **I-43** A grant is published at the lower of the programme maximum and the grantee's current choice, and is hidden when no choice exists.
- The public summary is written or approved by the grantee. It is never the proposal text.
- Public pages carry no tracking scripts and set no cookies.
- A machine-readable feed of the public data is available when P-52 is on. It applies the same filter as the pages.

NOTE 1: Public opt-in does not affect reporting to the funder. Periodic reporting data covers every recipient, as the grant agreement requires.

NOTE 2: EU funding acknowledgement duties that the grant agreement passes on to recipients apply on the grantee's own channels, independent of this opt-in (L-08).

NOTE 3: The granting authority may use non-sensitive materials it receives from the beneficiaries, such as summaries and deliverables, for its own publicity (MGA Article 16.3). The opt-in screen tells grantees that their choice governs this platform only.
