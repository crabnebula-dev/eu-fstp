# Complaints

Applicants and grantees can contest a procedural decision. Complaints cover how a decision was reached, not the reviewers' expert judgement of merit.

## Scope

In scope: knock-out decisions, conflict of interest handling, missed deadlines or notices, deviation from the published Guide, payment request rejections, bank detail holds, and refusals of hardship contributions.

Out of scope: disagreement with scores or expert opinion where the procedure was followed.

## Procedure (O-32)

1. **Complaint.** The complainant files within P-61 of the decision notice, through the platform.
2. **Coordinator review.** A coordinator staff member who took no part in the contested decision reviews it and responds within P-62.
3. **Escalation.** If not satisfied, the complainant escalates within P-63. At least two IRC members review the case and respond within P-64. Their decision is final within the programme.

## States

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| FILED | Received, scope check pending |
| OUT_OF_SCOPE | Concerns merit only; reasoned reply sent |
| COORDINATOR_REVIEW | Under first-level review |
| RESOLVED | Complainant accepted the response, or the escalation window lapsed |
| ESCALATED | Under IRC review |
| UPHELD | Procedural error confirmed; remedy recorded |
| DISMISSED | No procedural error found |

## Remedies

An upheld complaint leads to one of: re-evaluation by different reviewers in the same call if its timeline allows, re-entry into the next call at the stage where the error occurred, or correction of the contested payment decision. Remedies never reduce another applicant's awarded grant.

## Rules

- **SoD-13** Nobody who took part in the contested decision reviews the complaint at either level. An IRC member who validated the contested proposal does not review its escalation.
- **I-56** Every complaint receives a reasoned, recorded response within the applicable deadline, and each deadline is a timeline event with escalation to the programme manager.
- **I-57** Filing a complaint never changes the complainant's evaluation, scores or payment status, except through a recorded remedy.
- Complaint records stay visible to the complainant, reviewers of the complaint and auditors only. They are part of the audit file per call (`03-architecture/funder-reporting.md`).
- The Guide for Applicants describes this procedure (section 14).

NOTE: Complaints arise under the subgrant contract and the programme rules. Recipients are not parties to the grant agreement, so any further remedy depends on the law governing the subgrant contract (L-14).
