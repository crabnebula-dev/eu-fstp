# Support programme

Grantees receive support from the consortium alongside their funding (P-60). For SuperSAFE this is:

| Element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Mentoring | A consortium expert is assigned to a grant on request or at kick-off |
| Presenting results | Grantees can present work results to the consortium in scheduled sessions |
| Promotion and visibility | The consortium promotes grants through its channels, within the grantee's publication choice |
| CRA compliance guidance | Guidance on Cyber Resilience Act obligations relevant to the grantee's software |

## Rules

- Support is provided by consortium partners at no cost to the grantee. It is not part of the grant amount and never a condition for payment.
- **SoD-12** A mentor of a grant is never an assigned reviewer for later proposals from the same grantee team, and never accepts payment requests for the grant they mentor.
- **I-54** Every mentoring assignment is recorded as a relationship in the identity vault and is part of conflict detection in all later calls of the programme.
- Promotion uses only what the grantee has opted in to publish (I-43). Promotion outside the platform follows the same choice.
- Presentations are internal to the consortium unless the grantee agrees otherwise. Materials the grantee shares may reach the granting authority through reporting (MGA 16.3), and the grantee is told so.
- **I-55** No consortium partner makes commercial offers to grantees through the platform, and grantee contact data is never used for commercial purposes.

NOTE 1: I-55 matters because consortium partners, CrabNebula included, may offer paid services in the same fields as the support programme, such as CRA compliance. Keeping support free and unconditional supports the no-benefit guarantee in Annexe section 8.a and MGA Article 12.

NOTE 2: Partner effort on the support programme is eligible only if Annex 1 describes it as action tasks (L-13).

## Platform support

- Mentor assignment per grant, with acceptance by the mentor and the grantee.
- Mentoring sessions coordinated in the project group (`03-architecture/messaging.md`). Session notes are optional and private to mentor and grantee.
- Presentation slots scheduled through the timeline engine, with reminders.
- Grantees can end a mentoring assignment at any time.
