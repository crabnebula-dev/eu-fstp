What happens after you request activation

  1. A tenant admin who did not build this programme approves it, with a passkey confirmation (I-67, I-78). You cannot choose the approver.
  2. A tenant admin grants each role once its holder has accepted the terms and verified their identity (SoD-09).
  3. The call drafter drafts the first call. A programme manager who did not draft it approves it (R-PC-03).
  4. Publishing the call generates the Guide for Applicants and freezes the call's rules (I-52, I-12).

Changes after the request: Any change to the draft withdraws the request. The report runs again, and the warnings need a new acknowledgement.
