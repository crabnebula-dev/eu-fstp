Help for this stage

Draft (DRAFT)

Voyage pack label: At the departure hall

What is happening now

You write your proposal. Only your team sees it.

Who acts

You and your team.

Deadline

The call deadline.

What you can do

Edit, run the pre-submission check, submit or withdraw.

Submitted (SUBMITTED)

Voyage pack label: Departed, awaiting border check

What is happening now

The FSTP coordinator checks the knock-out (eligibility) criteria.

Who acts

The FSTP coordinator.

Deadline

No fixed deadline. You get an activity entry when it is done.

What you can do

Wait, or withdraw.

Stage 1 evaluation (STAGE1)

Voyage pack label: Crossing

What is happening now

Assigned reviewers score your proposal.

Who acts

Assigned reviewers.

Deadline

Stage 1 closes when the scores are complete.

What you can do

Wait, or withdraw.

Stage 2 questions (STAGE2)

Voyage pack label: Ascent: questions from the scouts

What is happening now

Reviewers asked clarification questions. They rescore your proposal after you answer.

Who acts

You answer. Assigned reviewers then score.

Your window closes in 8 days

12 Nov 2024, 17:00 Europe/Brussels (17:00 Europe/Malta, your zone).

What you can do

Answer the questions, upload a revised plan, or withdraw.

Ranked, awaiting committee decision (RANKED)

Voyage pack label: Awaiting the council

What is happening now

The committee ranks all proposals of the call within the remaining budget.

Who acts

The selection committee.

Deadline

The cut-off round date.

What you can do

Wait, or withdraw.

Independent review (IRC_REVIEW)

Voyage pack label: Independent watch

What is happening now

The Independent Review Committee checks the nominated proposal and its outcome.

Who acts

IRC members.

Deadline

No fixed deadline.

What you can do

Wait, or withdraw.

Negotiation (NEGOTIATION)

Voyage pack label: Treaty

What is happening now

Identity check in the vault, grant amount and grant agreement.

Who acts

You and the programme manager.

Deadline

Shown on the negotiation page.

What you can do

Verify identity, read the data protection notice, review the amount, or withdraw.

Granted (GRANTED)

Voyage pack label: Charter in force

What is happening now

The grant agreement is in force.

Who acts

You and the programme manager.

Deadline

The first milestone start date.

What you can do

Prepare your first milestone.

Project in progress (IN_EXECUTION)

Voyage pack label: On the road

What is happening now

You deliver milestones and request payments.

Who acts

You, the programme manager and finance.

Deadline

Each milestone due date.

What you can do

Submit evidence and payment requests.

Completed (COMPLETED)

Voyage pack label: Safe harbour

What is happening now

All milestones closed and all payment requests settled.

Who acts

Nobody. The process has ended.

Deadline

None.

What you can do

Download your proposal history (PDF).

Deferred to the next call (DEFERRED)

Voyage pack label: Wintering for the next voyage

What is happening now

The proposal waits for the next call of the programme. It re-enters at the stage it left, STAGE2 or IRC_REVIEW. The original call's policy still applies.

Who acts

Nobody. The system moves it when the next call opens.

Deadline

The next call opening date.

What you can do

Wait, or withdraw.

Not eligible (INELIGIBLE)

Voyage pack label: Route closed at the border

What is happening now

The proposal did not pass a knock-out criterion. It cannot continue in this call.

Who acts

Recorded by the FSTP coordinator with a reason.

Deadline

Complaint window per the call policy.

What you can do

Read the reason. Make a complaint if you think it is wrong.
