# Horizon Europe grant agreement alignment

Checked against the Horizon Europe General MGA v1.2 (01.06.2026) and the AGA v2.0 (01.04.2025). The AGA retrieval covered its introduction, Data Sheet and Articles 1 to 6.1. Articles 17.2, 20 and 21 and the AGA annotations on financial support to third parties were not retrieved and remain to be checked (L-12).

All entries paraphrase the source. Exact wording must come from the signed SuperSAFE grant agreement.
