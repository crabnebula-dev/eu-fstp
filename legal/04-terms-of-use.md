## 1. Who we are

The platform is operated by CrabNebula Ltd, TODO(legal): registered address and company number. Each funding programme on the platform is run by an organisation, called the "programme operator" here. The programme operator, not CrabNebula Ltd, decides on proposals, grants and payments.

## 2. Your account

- You create an account with your email address and a passkey. Keep your devices and passkeys safe.
- Your account belongs to one programme operator's space on the platform. An account in another operator's space is a separate account.
- Do not share your account. Do not act in someone else's name.
- Tell the programme operator at once if you think someone else used your account.

## 3. What you may do

You may use the platform to apply for funding, to work on a team's proposal, to carry out a role you were invited to, and to manage a grant. You may connect an AI agent to act for you, within the limits the platform shows when you connect it. You stay responsible for what the agent does in your name.

## 4. What you must not do

- Give false information, including about your identity, age, country or affiliations.
- Submit content that you have no right to submit.
- Hide instructions aimed at the AI assessor in a proposal. Reviewers see such attempts.
- Try to read data your role does not allow, or to get around rate limits or security controls.
- Send malware, or overload or probe the service.
- Use the platform for any unlawful purpose.

## 5. Your content

You keep the rights in what you submit. You allow the programme operator and the platform to store, process and show your content as the programme needs. This includes giving it to assigned reviewers and to the AI assessor. Public pages show only what you choose to publish.

## 6. Decisions and complaints

The programme operator takes every decision on your proposal, grant and payments. A named person records it. AI findings never decide.

If you think a decision was reached the wrong way, you can file a complaint on the platform. The Guide for Applicants of the call sets out the steps and deadlines. Disagreement with reviewers' expert scores is not grounds for a complaint.
